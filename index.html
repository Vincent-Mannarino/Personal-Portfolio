Why mechatronics

Coding, circuits, and math, all in one place

My interest in engineering started with Scratch in elementary school, moved into Python and then C++ once I started wiring up Arduinos for a STEM fair solar tracker. What I liked most was watching code I wrote on a screen actually move something in the real world — that's what pulled me toward circuits, sensors, and eventually machine learning. Mechatronics is the program that lets me keep all three: software, electronics, and mechanical systems, together.
